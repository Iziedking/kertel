# Track A demo walkthrough

Positioning: Telt makes a trading decision inspectable: what it read, why it paid, what it concluded, and what the user approved. Avoid claiming no other agent does this.

Suggested 90-second recording:

1. 0–12s: Homepage. State the problem: confident trading answers often hide the evidence trail.
2. 12–32s: Compare the two labelled fixture questions. Show the source/cost difference and open the actual research receipt.
3. 32–48s: Show the stored NO_TRADE conclusion, evidence citations and invalidation condition. More data does not force an entry.
4. 48–66s: In your MCP client, show a decision tied to a proposal in fixture mode. Explain Binance Agent OS and the exact approval boundary.
5. 66–82s: Verify the sample receipt, tamper with its conclusion, then verify again. The signature fails. Payment/order/content/timestamp claims remain independently unverified.
6. 82–90s: End with the repository and reproducibility commands.

The browser conclusion is scripted. Distinguish fixtures, live read-only observations and real execution. Do not show credentials or real pending confirmation codes. This walkthrough does not require a live trade.
